How the prediction works

Each discipline is projected independently using Riegel's endurance formula: T₂ = T₁ × (D₂ / D₁)k. The exponent k is slightly above 1 (swim 1.02, bike 1.04, run 1.06) to account for the natural slow‑down as distance grows.

Every past race is projected to your target distance, then the projections are combined with a recency weighting (more recent races count more; 12‑month half‑life). Transitions are averaged from your history. More races → a more reliable prediction.

Data is stored only in your browser (localStorage). Estimates are for guidance, not guarantees. Train smart & race safe. 🏁
